PackPrint for Windows

Watches your eBay orders and auto-prints packing slips the moment payment clears.

Download for Windows

v0.1.0 · Windows 10/11, 64-bit · includes your license key

Setup

  1. Unzip the download -- it contains the installer and a LICENSE-KEY.txt file.
  2. Run PackPrint Setup 0.1.0.exe and follow the installer.
  3. Open PackPrint. On first launch, paste the key from LICENSE-KEY.txt into the activation screen.
  4. Click Sign in with eBay to connect your seller account, then set up your printer under Printer.
