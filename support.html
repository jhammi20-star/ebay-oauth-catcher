PackPrint Support

Watches your eBay orders and auto-prints packing slips the moment payment clears.

Getting started

Open PackPrint, click "Sign in with eBay" in the eBay Connection section, approve access, and paste the code back in. Then pick your packing-list printer under Printer and click Test Print to confirm it looks right.

Common questions

Nothing is printing. Check the Activity log at the bottom of the app -- it explains why each check was skipped (not connected, printer not set, or polling paused). Make sure "Pause / Resume Auto-Polling" reads "Running," not "Paused."

My eBay connection stopped working. Click "Reconnect / Change" under eBay Connection in Settings and sign in again -- eBay sign-ins periodically need renewing.

The packing slip is the wrong size. Check Paper Size under the Printer section matches the label stock loaded in your printer.
